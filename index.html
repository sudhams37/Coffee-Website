About-Us

Coffee, one of the world’s most loved beverages, originated in Ethiopia in the 9th century and has since grown into a global tradition. It comes from the seeds of coffee cherries, commonly called beans, with Arabica, Robusta, Liberica, and Excelsa being the main varieties. Arabica is smooth and aromatic, while Robusta is stronger, more bitter, and higher in caffeine; Liberica offers rare fruity notes, and Excelsa adds a tart, complex flavor. The journey from bean to cup includes cultivation in tropical regions, careful harvesting, processing, roasting, and brewing through methods like espresso, French press, or pour-over.
